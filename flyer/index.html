Call for Papers & Submissions

Key Topics (Indicative — All Communications-Theory Work Welcome)
  • Information theory and coding
  • Non-terrestrial and satellite links
  • Wireless & cellular systems (including 5G/6G/B6G)
  • Physical-layer security and privacy
  • Signal processing for communications
  • AI/Machine learning for communications
  • Network information theory and distributed systems
  • Quantum communications
  • mmWave and THz systems
  • Wireless and passive sensing

Papers will be presented in poster sessions, allowing maximum interaction between researchers and workshop delegates. AusCTW prioritises poster presentations to maximise discussion and collaboration. We invite you to prepare an abstract summarising your work or work-in-progress.

Abstract Guidelines:

  • Length: Approximately 250–300 words (brief and self-contained).
  • Deadline: Expected to be early January 2027 (exact date to be announced), to give us time to typeset and print the booklet of abstracts for distribution at the workshop.
  • Submission: Abstracts can be submitted via the following form: https://forms.gle/A47ohieQaCDS9r6F9

Keynotes & Tutorials

If you have a proposal for a keynote or tutorial oral presentation, get in touch by email at ausctw@gmail.com with the subject line “[AusCTW2027 talk]”. Time is limited!

Similarly, if you know someone who could give a talk of interest to our community, let us know and we can reach out to them. Thank you!
